Parentuide Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This document describes the data collected through the Parentuide website, particularly the survey forms for parents and professionals. Before collecting data, the controller should verify this information against the actual website configuration, provider agreements and retention settings.

1. Data controller

The data controller is m2Investments sp. z o.o., Domaniewska 37/2.43, 02-672 Warsaw, Poland. For privacy-related enquiries, contact: kontakt.m2investments@gmail.com.

2. What data we may process

  • answers submitted in the parent or professional survey;
  • an email address, if voluntarily provided by the user;
  • information about consent to be contacted and data needed to document and manage that consent;
  • technical data related to operating and securing the form, such as request information, receipt time or anti-spam verification results, to the extent these are actually logged by the services used.

Please do not include information in open-text answers that could identify children or other people, or detailed information about health, diagnoses or other sensitive matters.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Analysis of survey responses and user needs research — based on the controller’s legitimate interests in analysing needs and developing the Parentuide concept (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), if the controller considers this basis appropriate after a balancing assessment.
  • Contact about research interviews and platform testing — based on the consent of a person who voluntarily provided an email address and separately opted in to being contacted (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Consent to contact is not a condition of completing the survey.
  • Protecting forms against spam and abuse and maintaining security — based on the controller’s legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), if appropriate following a balancing assessment.
  • Establishing, pursuing or defending legal claims — where necessary, on the appropriate legal basis depending on the circumstances.

Before launching the surveys, the controller should confirm that these legal bases match the actual purposes and processing activities.

4. Whether providing data is voluntary

Participation in the survey is voluntary. Providing an email address and consenting to contact are optional. Not providing an email address should not prevent survey submission. If certain technical data are necessary to protect the form, processing them may be required to use the form.

5. Data recipients and service providers

Data may be processed by technology providers used to host the website, store responses and protect against spam, including:

  • GitHub Pages / GitHub — for hosting the public website;
  • Supabase — for backend services and the response database, depending on the project configuration;
  • Cloudflare Turnstile / Cloudflare — for anti-spam verification of the form.

The data shared with these providers depends on the website configuration and how their services operate. The controller should review the applicable agreements, data processing terms, subprocessors and any transfers outside the European Economic Area. Data may also be disclosed to competent authorities where required by law.

6. Data retention

  • Survey responses: for as long as needed to complete idea validation and analyse the results. They will then be deleted or irreversibly anonymised when no longer needed for these purposes, unless further retention is required by law or necessary to handle claims.
  • Email addresses and contact consent: until consent is withdrawn or the contact purpose ends, while retaining minimal information needed to document consent or its withdrawal where legally justified.
  • Technical and security data: for as long as necessary to protect forms, detect abuse and maintain security, according to the actual service configuration.

The controller should define and apply specific operational deletion periods, including for backups and service providers where possible.

7. Your data protection rights

Subject to the conditions set out in the GDPR, you may request access to your personal data, rectification, erasure or restriction of processing and, where applicable, data portability. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

Where processing is based on legitimate interests, you may object on grounds relating to your particular situation. You may also lodge a complaint with the President of the Polish Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

To exercise your rights, contact kontakt.m2investments@gmail.com.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

The website may use technical mechanisms necessary for its operation and third-party services such as anti-spam verification. Do not assume the website uses no cookies or similar technologies without checking the current hosting, scripts and provider configuration. If analytics or advertising cookies are introduced, the controller should update this policy and obtain consent before they are activated where required by law.

9. Security

The controller applies technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of processing. Forms may use anti-spam checks and submission limits. However, no internet solution can guarantee complete security of data transmission or storage.

10. Automated decision-making

Survey responses are not intended to be used to make solely automated decisions about respondents that produce legal or similarly significant effects. This information should be updated if the website’s operation changes.

11. Changes to this privacy policy

This policy may be updated if the website, data processing activities or applicable laws change. The current version and its update date will be published at this address.
